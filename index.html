How it works & tips
  • Pulse (rPPG): the average colour of your forehead and cheeks changes very slightly with every heartbeat. The POS algorithm (Wang et al., 2017) separates that change from lighting and movement. Heart rate comes from the spectrum; beat-to-beat intervals come from peak detection.
  • Breathing: tracks tiny vertical movement of your chest/shoulders (and head) between frames. Textured or striped clothing helps; a plain flat shirt makes it harder.
  • HR wave (RSA) & resonance: heart rate naturally rises when you breathe in and falls when you breathe out. At about 6 breaths/min the wave gets large and regular. Resonance % is how much of the heart-rate variation sits in one clean rhythm (HeartMath-style coherence).
  • Face: tension = brow lowering + eye squint + lip pressing + nose wrinkle (MediaPipe blendshapes). Blink rate is counted per minute.
  • Best results: stay still, light from the front (a window or lamp behind the camera), no backlight, avoid flickering lights, and try Lock exposure.
  • Ground truth: Connect HR strap pairs any Bluetooth heart-rate strap (Polar H10 recommended; Chrome/Edge only) so you can compare the camera against real beat-to-beat data.
  • Keys: P pacer, M marker, R record. The HRV numbers from the camera are experimental; trust the strap for HRV.
